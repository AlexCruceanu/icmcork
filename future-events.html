Coming this winter

Intercultural Christmas Market 2026

✦

The market returns to Little Island for its fourth edition, with a full day of food, gifts, traditions and festive cheer from communities all over the world.

  • Date Sunday 15th November 2026
  • Time 10.40am to 4.30pm
  • Location Little Island Sports Complex T45 F449

What to Expect

Every stall at the market has a story behind it. Walk the hall and you can taste dishes from one country, pick up a handmade gift from another, and chat with the people who made them, all under one roof.

It is a great day out for all ages, and a chance to get your Christmas shopping started early while supporting small businesses and local makers.

Santa will be there too, so bring the little ones along to say hello!
